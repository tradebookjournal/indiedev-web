# TradeBook Journal — Account & Data Deletion

## Request deletion

TradeBook Journal allows users to permanently delete their TradeBook account data directly from the app.

To delete your TradeBook account:

1. Open TradeBook Journal.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Review the deletion information.
5. Confirm the deletion.

After confirmation, TradeBook permanently deletes the TradeBook data associated with your account.

## What is deleted

Account deletion removes:

- Trading records
- Journal entries
- Playbook rules
- TradeBook's local backup stored on the device
- TradeBook's Google Drive backup

Deleted TradeBook data cannot be recovered.

## What is not deleted

Deleting your TradeBook account does **not** delete your Google account.

Your Google account remains available and can be used to sign in to other Google services.

If you sign in to TradeBook again with the same Google account, your previous TradeBook data will not be restored after deletion.

## Data retention

TradeBook does not intentionally retain your deleted TradeBook trading data after an account deletion request is completed, except where retention is required by applicable law or necessary for security and fraud prevention.

## Need help?

If you cannot access the app or cannot complete the deletion process, contact us using the support contact provided on the TradeBook Journal Google Play listing.
