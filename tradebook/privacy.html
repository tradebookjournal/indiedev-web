TradeBook Journal Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

On this page

TradeBook Journal ("TradeBook", "we", "us", or "our") is a trading journal application designed to help users record, review, and learn from their trading activity.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TradeBook accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how users can delete their TradeBook data.

1. Information we access

When you use TradeBook, the app may access the following information:

Google account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, TradeBook receives account information provided by Google for authentication, such as:

  • Google account email address
  • Google account profile information made available through Google Sign-In
  • Authentication information required to maintain your TradeBook session

TradeBook uses this information to identify your TradeBook account.

TradeBook does not use your Google account to access unrelated Google services.

Trading and journal information

TradeBook may store information that you enter into the application, including:

  • Trade records
  • Entry and exit information
  • Trade notes
  • Journal entries
  • Trading rules and playbook information
  • Performance and analytics information derived from your trading records

This information is used to provide the core functionality of TradeBook.

Backup information

If you use TradeBook backup features, your TradeBook data may be stored in:

  • Local application storage on your device
  • A local TradeBook backup file on your device
  • Your Google Drive through TradeBook's Google Drive backup feature

Google Drive access is used only to provide the TradeBook backup and restore functionality that you request.

2. How we use information

TradeBook uses information to:

  • Provide account authentication
  • Store and display your trading records
  • Provide journal and playbook functionality
  • Calculate trading performance and analytics
  • Provide backup and restore functionality
  • Maintain your TradeBook account
  • Respond to support requests
  • Protect the application from misuse and security threats

TradeBook does not sell your personal information.

TradeBook does not use your trading records for advertising personalization.

3. Google services

TradeBook uses Google Sign-In for authentication.

If you choose to use Google Drive backup, TradeBook also uses Google Drive APIs to create, update, retrieve, and delete TradeBook backup data in your Google Drive.

Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

TradeBook does not delete your Google Account when you delete your TradeBook account.

4. Data storage

TradeBook is designed to keep your trading data associated with your TradeBook account.

Account-specific TradeBook data is separated so that one TradeBook account does not intentionally access another TradeBook account's trading records.

Some application preferences, such as theme and currency preferences, may be stored locally on the device and are not treated as account trading data.

5. Data sharing

TradeBook does not sell your personal information.

TradeBook may use third-party services necessary to provide application functionality, including:

  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Google Drive for optional TradeBook backups

Information shared with these services is limited to what is necessary for the relevant functionality.

6. Data security

TradeBook uses reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the application.

Authentication with Google is handled through Google's authentication services.

Data transmitted to supported online services is sent using secure HTTPS connections.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data retention

TradeBook keeps your TradeBook data while you maintain your TradeBook account or until you delete it.

When you request deletion of your TradeBook account through the supported deletion process, TradeBook is designed to delete the TradeBook data associated with that account, including:

  • Trades
  • Journal entries
  • Playbook rules
  • Local TradeBook backup data
  • TradeBook Google Drive backup data

Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with applicable law, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security of the service.

If information must be retained for one of these reasons, it will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for that purpose.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your TradeBook account from within the application.

Open:

Settings → Delete Account

TradeBook requires confirmation before account deletion is completed.

After deletion, the TradeBook data associated with the account is permanently removed and cannot be recovered through TradeBook.

Deleting your TradeBook account does not delete your Google Account.

You may sign in to TradeBook again with the same Google Account after deletion, but previously deleted TradeBook data will not automatically return.

If you cannot access the application, you can use the TradeBook account deletion web resource provided on the TradeBook Journal Google Play listing.

9. Children's privacy

TradeBook is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the services in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TradeBook's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or TradeBook's data practices, please contact the developer through the support contact provided on the TradeBook Journal Google Play listing.
